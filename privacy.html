neoQuad (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by neoQuad when you use our app and website.

We don't sell your data.

Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.

Encrypted, end to end.

Passwords are hashed. Traffic is encrypted in transit and at rest.

You stay in control.

Access, update, export, or delete your data at any time.

Built for .edu.

Designed for students 13 and over with a valid school email.

01

Information we collect.

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account or use our services:

  • Name and username
  • Email address (your .edu email)
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Photos you choose to upload
02

How we use your information.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Connect you with campus events and clubs
  • Send you notifications about events you've RSVP'd to
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Analyze usage to improve the app experience
03

Information sharing.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the app (hosting, analytics)
  • Other users when you RSVP to events (your name may be visible)
  • Law enforcement when required by law
04

Data security.

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encryption and secure servers. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

05

Your rights.

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of promotional communications
  • Request a copy of your data
06

Children's privacy.

neoQuad is designed for college students. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

07

Changes to this policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the “Last updated” date.

Questions about your privacy?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please get in touch — we read every message.
